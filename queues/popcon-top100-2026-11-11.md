# Debian Popcon top-100 Perl libraries: complete

All 100 of the most-installed `lib*-perl` packages in the 2026-11-11
Popcon snapshot map to a successful XPerl build record or an XPerl bundle:
98 have successful records, and two are bundled. The
[package mapping and counts](popcon-top100-2026-11-11.json) preserve the
selection. These are installation counts among participating systems,
not counts of CPAN downloads.

The follow-up batch installed all 17 previously unrecorded distributions,
then installed Locale::gettext. The latter's older failure did not recur
on the current installed interpreter. Only Dpkg needed a source patch.
Its [review](../data/87/GUILLEM/Dpkg/REVIEW.md) explains the general test
fixes, their evidence and the configuration for per-prefix data paths.

The 18 follow-up targets were tested with installed XPerl v5.45.5, commit
`8903238ce74fc124ec407b8342791ef73253f988`, x86_64 Linux, GCC 13.3.0,
`PERL_RC_STACK`, no threads, multiplicity or debugging, double NV,
without longdouble or quadmath. Tests used `PERL_DESTRUCT_LEVEL=2`.
The other 80 successful records span recorded build profiles; this report
does not claim that all 100 pass on every profile or operating system.

Glib::Object::Introspection needs the native GObject Introspection
development files. On the tested Ubuntu noble-based host, installing
`libgirepository1.0-dev` supplied the missing pkg-config entry and tools.
GTK 3 development files and typelibs were already present. Both
Glib::Object::Introspection and Gtk3 then installed without source patches.

The batch used four build workers and one Codex repair worker with
`gpt-6.1-sol`, high effort and a ten-minute timeout per repair call.
Dpkg's final fresh replay and Locale::gettext required no AI repair.
All build results were published through the normal installer workflow.
No active targets remain in this Popcon batch.
